# Open questions and research

These questions should remain explicit rather than being converted into facts:

- What exact postwar Multiphone model and production date is this unit?
- What are the terminal-strip assignments?
- What are the functions of the external two-screw block and unidentified jack?
- What does the Stancor transformer power?
- Does the cabinet contain a playback speaker, microphone, or both?
- Which request/program wiring arrangement applied to this generation?
- Are there surviving postwar service manuals or installation diagrams?
- How did Shyvers operators physically maintain each establishment's queue?
- Does a later utility patent describe a revised postwar electrical system?
- Can the original microphone and speaker be used safely through isolated audio
  interfaces?

## Research sources to pursue

- US 2,264,911, *Telephonic Music Selector Apparatus* (1941)

- D146,880 and D146,881, 1946 Music Box designs
