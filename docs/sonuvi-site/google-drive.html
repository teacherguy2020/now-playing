Google Drive

Browse and play the audio files you choose from Google Drive, right alongside your other Sonuvi libraries.

Connect when you choose

Google Drive is optional. When you connect it, Sonuvi uses Google’s supported sign-in and Drive APIs to browse the files and folders you select.

Your files stay yours

Sonuvi does not delete or modify your Google Drive files. Selected audio and artwork may be cached on your device to make playback responsive, and you can remove the library from Sonuvi without affecting the originals in Google Drive.
