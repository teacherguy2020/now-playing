Sonuvi Server

Sonuvi Server is an open-source enhancement layer for moOde Audio. It keeps moOde at the center of your listening system while adding a richer, more personal way to browse, play, and control your music.

Your library and playback remain on hardware you control. Sonuvi provides the web and mobile-friendly services around moOde without moving your music into a required cloud service.

What makes it different

  • A more capable music library. Browse, search, and organize artists, albums, playlists, favorites, ratings, and artwork from one consistent interface.
  • Queue Wizard and Vibe. Shape a queue from your library, discover a related mix, and keep the music moving without giving up control of the active moOde queue.
  • Radio that understands what is playing. Sonuvi can enrich supported classical and music-radio broadcasts with conservative metadata, artwork, Apple Music links, and station-aware display behavior.
  • One playback model across surfaces. The same server-side catalog, queue, history, and playback contracts support the web controller, native iPhone and iPad clients, CarPlay, and Alexa integrations.
  • Open and self-hosted. Sonuvi is designed to be inspected, adapted, and run with your own moOde system, while keeping credentials and internal server details behind authenticated APIs.

Built around your system

Sonuvi Server is not a replacement music store or a second music library. It works alongside moOde and MPD, preserving the server as the source of truth for your catalog and playback while adding the interfaces and services that make the system easier to live with.
